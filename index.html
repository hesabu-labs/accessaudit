How it works

  1. Pick a plan and enter the site URL at checkout. You confirm you own the site or have permission to scan it.
  2. We scan the site automatically, following links within the same domain.
  3. The report is generated and a download link arrives by email.

What this is, and is not

Automated tools find only part of the accessibility problems on a site. AccessAudit is a first-pass audit, not a certificate of compliance and not legal advice. Keyboard flow, reading order and the quality of alt text still need a human check.

We do not bypass bot protection. If a site blocks automated scans, the scan stops and you are refunded.

Questions

Can I resell the report to my client?

Yes. The report is yours to use for your own and your clients' sites.

What if my site blocks the scan?

If we cannot load your pages, for example behind a CAPTCHA or a bot challenge, you are refunded automatically. See the refund policy.

Do you scan pages behind a login?

No. Only publicly reachable pages are scanned.

Which standard do you check against?

The WCAG rules built into axe-core. Reports show which rule each issue breaks.
