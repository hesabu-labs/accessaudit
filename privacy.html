Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and the site URL you submit at checkout
  • Scan results for that site: page addresses and the accessibility issues found, including short snippets of the page code around each issue

Who processes it

  • Lemon Squeezy: payment and order data
  • GitHub: runs the scan
  • Groq: receives issue descriptions and code snippets to write plain-language explanations
  • Cloudflare: hosting and temporary report storage
  • Brevo: sends your email

How long we keep it

Report files are deleted 7 days after delivery. Order records are kept as needed for accounting and refunds.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
