Automatic refund

If we cannot scan your site, for example because it blocks automated access or the pages fail to load, the scan stops and your payment is refunded in full automatically. You will receive an email explaining what happened.

Report not delivered

If you paid and no report or refund reaches you, email hesabu-labs@pm.me with your order number and we will resolve it.

After delivery

Because the report is a digital product delivered immediately, we do not refund delivered reports, except where the report is clearly defective, such as an unreadable file. Contact us within 7 days of delivery.
